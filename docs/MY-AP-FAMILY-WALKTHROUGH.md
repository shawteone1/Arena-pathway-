# My AP — 7-minute Rivera family presentation

Positioning: **Keep the tools you already use. Manage their feeds in one actionable
dashboard.** Powerful underneath. Simple everywhere else.

Start at Arena Pathway's homepage on a private local preview. Select **SIEM**.
My AP opens the fictional Rivera family in My Day automatically. There is no role
landing-page choice or recruiting-profile requirement. Reset demo before starting.
This is the unchanged May 6–12, 2030 America/Denver fixture week: Alex soccer,
Jordan volleyball, Taylor soccer AND volleyball, four teams, two sports. All people,
clubs, venues and provider information are fictional; no real account is connected.

## 0:00–1:00 — My Day: the three questions

Point to the four primary sections: My Day, Calendar, Messages, My Family.
My Day answers what's next, what's changed and what needs me. Jordan has Monday
volleyball at 4:45 PM; Alex's changed soccer practice is 5:15 PM; Taylor has
volleyball at 6:30 PM. Source freshness distinguishes fixture age from view refresh.
The banner states that all data is fictional and real providers are unconnected.

Say: “One family view, without replacing the tools the clubs already use.”

## 1:00–2:00 — Review an overlap and a change

Open the Monday conflict action. Alex and Jordan overlap, and Taylor's 6:10 PM
arrival competes with Jordan's 6:15 PM pickup. The next step is to confirm coverage,
not automatically move a schedule. Close details and open Alex's practice change
in the queue or What’s changed panel. Show the earlier 4:30 PM/Pine Grove plan and
the 5:15 PM/Willow Creek plan.

Original simulated sources retain the PlayMetrics update, TeamSnap calendar copy
and Slack announcement. It is one mapped practice and one change decision with
three source records, not three duplicate alerts. Jordan's Wednesday update is a
different real-in-the-fixture change. Dates/times/child/team scope stay inspectable.

## 2:00–3:00 — Messages: useful context, not another inbox

Select Messages. Show the Slack change announcement, actionable transport reminder,
PlayMetrics team notice and TeamSnap RSVP reminder. Open related activity details.
Source attribution and timestamps remain attached; nothing retrieves private chats.
Slack is a ten-minute-old snapshot against a five-minute fictional threshold,
so it is stale. A view refresh does not synchronize any provider.

Say: “Keep the tools. Bring the relevant context to the next decision.”

## 3:00–4:15 — Calendar: the whole week, focused when needed

Select Calendar. All 15 commitments and two deadlines appear together, including
school, family dinner, team meeting, practices, soccer matches and the volleyball
tournament. On a phone the Calendar section shows the full list; My Day remains
a compact preview with Show more disclosures.

Focus on Taylor; soccer and volleyball both appear. Use More filters → Sport →
Volleyball, then clear. Use Teams to focus Jordan; clear. Source filtering retains
secondary evidence matches: a practice can still appear through its TeamSnap copy
while its primary PlayMetrics attribution remains intact. Clear filters.

Open Saturday's Jordan tournament. Travel plan: leave 6:30 AM, arrive 7:15 AM,
start at 8 AM. These are fictional parent plans, not traffic estimates. Alex's
9 AM soccer game and Taylor's 11:15 AM match explain Saturday's coverage conflict.

## 4:15–5:15 — A next step with explicit approval

Open the Friday tournament RSVP deadline from Calendar. It links the fictional
TeamSnap request to the SportsEngine tournament. Preview simulated action. The
approval button is disabled until its explicit checkbox is checked. If approved,
SIEM confirms that nothing was sent, submitted or changed in a provider.

Alternatively return to My Day and preview the Slack carpool action. Casey covers
Alex; Drew is asked to confirm Jordan pickup and Taylor arrival. Local resolution
records a queue decision only. It does not confirm a real ride or send a reply.
State remains coherent when moving between My Day, Calendar and Messages.

## 5:15–6:00 — My Family and independent products

Select My Family. Show Alex, Jordan and Taylor, their two sports and four teams.
No statistics, biographies, birthdays or recruiting profile are needed. Existing
local family setup/preferences remain available through the explicitly separate
browser-local tools link, without copying Rivera into personal records.

The Locked In reminder on Tuesday is minimal simulated status only. No identity,
health, payment or document records enter SIEM; no form is actually submitted.
Recruiting remains optional. Neither is a prerequisite for My AP scheduling.

## 6:00–7:00 — Optional modes and a safe shared screen

The same My AP experience offers Everyday, Command and Display presentation modes.
Choose Display; shared-screen privacy turns on automatically. Names, locations,
messages and private evidence disappear, while commitments and priority counts
remain glanceable. Family and Messages sections respect that privacy too.

Settings keeps connection status and optional role demos available without making
them primary navigation. The demo has no production login or live integrations.
Close: “What’s next? What’s changed? What needs me? Powerful underneath. Simple
everywhere else.” Reset demo to restore fixture actions and approval state.
