# Acquisition v2 — baseline failures and pre-evaluation protocol

Base: Sports Discovery Engine v1 `5d554b9`, itself based on integrated RC `50ce75d`. Existing registry, normalization, extraction, graph, reconciliation, review and tests were inspected before coding. All prior research/source links remain preserved.

The v1 work proved retention and configured extraction, not broad acquisition. Its organization-shaped raw candidates can be league participants, aliases or visiting meet teams. A largely blocked RMR gateway with a discovery-only adapter could not yield its member clubs. Lacrosse had no general source acquisition expansion beyond its small reviewed seeds and schedules. Manual source/program configuration restricted publication. Archive replay and synthetic plugins could not establish live recall. Source counts, memberships, typed programs and visible cards were different denominators. No independent held-out recall benchmark or operational acquisition console existed.

V2 will use the existing engine, client/access controls and review model. It will separately measure prescribed publisher capture, live bounded source discovery, controlled repeatability and actual user-visible coverage. Limits and failed thresholds will be delivered as results, not concealed behind passing unit tests.

Thresholds and rules were recorded before benchmark evaluation in `data/acquisition-v2/protocol.json`. Benchmark names/URLs will not be fed as an answer list to the independent acquisition job. A tool-assisted generic web-search export may provide source leads; unattended search-provider integration is a separate operational requirement. Independent benchmark assembly and a held-out split will be frozen before scoring. Same-analyst research is not an external blind audit.

The consumer application, branding, navigation, SIEM and offerings remain unchanged in this phase. Collective Data and open-ended Build My Pathway discovery are not activated.
