ABOUT US & MISSION

Your sporting life
doesn't belong

PlayRcmd is a technology venture from LA02UK, bringing a focused experience to the shared sports-information foundation.

Our mission is to simplify the sporting experience by bringing information, schedules, communications and opportunities from disconnected platforms into one manageable, actionable view for athletes, parents and coaches.

Our principle is simple: complement, never replace. Keep the tools your teams already trust. Connect authorized information. Reduce noise. Give people time back.
